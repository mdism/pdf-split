Questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is split by your own browser and never leaves your device. Nothing is saved.

How do I pick pages?

Type pages and ranges like 1-3, 5 to extract them into one PDF, or choose to save each range, every page or every N pages as separate files.

Can I make the pages smaller?

Yes. Choose Compress, and pick greyscale or black and white for the smallest files. Enhance cleans up faint scans.

Privacy policy

PDF Split · tools.shaikhismail.com/pdf-split

✓
We save nothing. Your PDFs are processed on your device and never uploaded. Anything held while processing is temporary and is deleted as soon as it is done, or when you close the tab.

No sign-up, no analytics, no ads.

The short version

PDF Split runs entirely in your web browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never shared with anyone.

There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. The only outside service is Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly bot check, explained below. It never sees your PDFs.

What happens to your PDFs

Your PDFs are read and processed by JavaScript in your browser, and the result is offered to you directly. Nothing is sent to a server.

If anything has to be held while it is being processed, it is held only temporarily in your browser's memory for that tab, and it is deleted as soon as processing finishes, when you clear the list, or when you close or reload the tab. Download links are cancelled a few seconds after you click them.

The page's security policy only lets it talk to this website and that verification service, and the only thing it ever sends is the one-time bot-check token. Your PDFs are never part of any request.

What is stored on your device

Only one thing, and only if you use it: when you switch between light and dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it any time from your browser settings.

What the web host sees

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub automatically records basic technical data when a page loads, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and protect its service. GitHub handles that under the GitHub privacy statement. It never includes your PDFs or anything you type into the tool, and we do not receive it.

Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To keep bots and spammers out, the tool asks for a quick Cloudflare Turnstile check before you can use it. Cloudflare looks at technical signals from your browser, such as your IP address and browser details, to tell people from bots. It does not see your PDFs or anything you type into the tool.

When the check passes, your browser gets a one-time token. Our small verification service, which also runs on Cloudflare, sends that token to Cloudflare to confirm it, gets a yes or no back, and keeps nothing. The token is not stored and is not linked to you. Cloudflare handles its part under the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Children

The tool does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
